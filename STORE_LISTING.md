## 5. Google Play Console Declarations Quick-Reference

- **Target Audience:** Age **13 and older** (Teen / Everyone 10+). Do **not** select children under 13.
- **Ads:** Select **Yes, my app contains ads** (due to AdMob rewarded video passes).
- **Financial Features:** Select **My app doesn't provide any financial features**.
- **Health / Medical:** Select **My app does not provide medical or health advice**.
- **Generative AI:** Select **Yes**, and confirm the app has in-app reporting (Report button in reading sheet) and strict prompt safety filtering.
- **App Access:** Select **All functionality is available without restrictions** (or provide standard guest access instructions: *"All divination sanctuaries and guest readings are accessible immediately upon opening the app without requiring an account or credentials"*).

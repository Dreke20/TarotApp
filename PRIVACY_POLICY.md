# Privacy Policy for Oraculum: Sacred AI Tarot

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

Welcome to **Oraculum: Sacred AI Tarot** ("Oraculum", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and spiritual sanctity. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and associated services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

#### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** If you choose to sign in using email or OAuth, we collect your email address and authentication identifiers.
- **Seeker Moniker & Ephemeris Data:** When you configure your sacred initiation or birth chart, you may input an optional display moniker, birth date, time, and birthplace for astronomical ephemeris calculations (Sun, Moon, Rising sign).
- **Tarot Invocations & Intentions:** Questions, journal reflections, and custom spread titles you submit during your personal divination sessions.

#### B. On-Device Guest Readings (Zero-Cloud Privacy)
If you use Oraculum as an anonymous seeker/guest, your tarot questions, card draws, reflections, and dream journal entries remain **strictly on your physical device** in secure local storage. They are never transmitted to our persistent database servers.

#### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- Device model, operating system version, and general diagnostic crash logs for stability and bug remediation.
- Haptic and accelerometer sensor input is processed ephemerally on-device to render real-time holographic 3D card tilt and is never recorded or transmitted.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
- To generate personalized AI tarot interpretations and astrological ephemeris calculations.
- To synchronize your reading history and favorite cards across devices if signed in.
- To manage native Google Play In-App Purchases and Oracle Pro subscriptions.
- To deliver non-intrusive rewarded starlight pass offerings via Google AdMob (for free-tier seekers).

---

### 3. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Processing
- **AI Synthesis:** Channeled readings are processed via secure Supabase Edge Functions interfacing with Google Gemini AI. Only the specific cards drawn, question text, and relevant astrological placements are passed to generate the reading.
- **No Training on Personal Data:** Your personal inquiries are not used to train public machine learning foundation models.
- **AdMob & In-App Purchases:** In-app purchases are securely processed by the Google Play Store billing service. We never collect or store your payment card numbers or banking credentials.

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion
- You may clear your local reading history at any time from within the app settings.
- If you have an authenticated account, you can request full account and data deletion by contacting us or via the Account Settings panel.

Oraculum is intended for individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 • Application ID: oraculum.dirtysouth

1. Overview & Sacred Trust

At Oraculum (developed by Oraculum Studios / Whispers & Warnings), your spiritual inquiries, personal journal entries, and astrological data are treated with sacred confidentiality. This policy outlines how information is handled across our Android mobile app and associated cloud services.

2. Data We Collect & How We Handle It

  • Spiritual Inquiries & Cards: When you consult the Oracle, your selected tarot archetypes and question text are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS/TLS to our secure Supabase Edge Functions to generate empathetic interpretations with Google Vertex AI. Questions are never used to train generalized third-party machine learning models.
  • Astrological Profile: Your date of birth, time, and geographic coordinates are stored locally on your device in secure storage (with HMAC cryptographic verification) to calculate your Natal Ephemeris. We do not sell or monetize personal birth charts.
  • Microphone & Camera Permissions:
    • Microphone: Used exclusively during the active session when you choose to use the Conversational Voice Oracle via Android SpeechRecognizer. Audio is processed on-device or via secure system pipes; raw audio recordings are never stored on remote servers.
    • Camera: Used strictly during active sessions in the AR Altar and Tarot Lens features to project spreads into your physical space. No camera video streams or frames are uploaded or saved.

3. Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

Oraculum utilizes Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide optional rewarded offerings (e.g. watching a video to earn Starlight Shards) and discreet banner placements for non-paying users:

  • AdMob may collect diagnostic identifiers and device metrics in compliance with Google Play Store Policies.
  • We enforce strict frequency caps to preserve a peaceful sanctuary.
  • Subscribers to Oracle Pro enjoy an entirely 100% ad-free experience.

4. Data Storage, Security & Row Level Security (RLS)

All cloud databases employ PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database kernel. A seeker can only read or modify records linked strictly to their authenticated account identifier (auth.uid()). Data in transit is secured with TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the absolute right under GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Data Safety policies to access, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated readings. You can initiate instant deletion within the app or through our dedicated web portal:

Oraculum is designed for general audiences and spiritual seekers aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA.

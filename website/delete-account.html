At Oraculum, we respect your spiritual autonomy and privacy rights. In full compliance with Google Play Store Data Safety Policies, GDPR (Article 17 - Right to Erasure), and CCPA (Right to Delete), you maintain total sovereignty over your account credentials, birth charts, tarot reading history, and stored preferences.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Oraculum application installed on your Android device, you can permanently erase your account and all associated cloud and local data instantly:

  1. Open the Oraculum app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Seeker Profile Avatar (Zodiac celestial icon) in the upper-left corner of the main altar screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the profile dialog and tap the crimson `DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA` button.
  4. Review the warning dialog detailing the permanent purge.
  5. Confirm by tapping `DELETE EVERYTHING`.

Immediate Outcome: Your session is permanently closed, all Supabase authentication records and profile entries are deleted, all tarot invocations are expunged, and local device keys are destroyed.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Without the App)

If you have uninstalled the app or do not have access to your Android device, submit your deletion request below. We will process the purge from our cloud servers and dispatch a formal confirmation receipt.
